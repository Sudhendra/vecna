What Makes Vecna Different

The AI that cares

Other AI tools respond when prompted. Vecna learns who you are, evolves with you, and works for you — even when you walk away.

✦

It Learns You

Vecna builds a living model of who you are. Your communication style, your preferences, the topics you care about, your emotional patterns. Not a settings page — it learns by listening.

It Grows With You

Every conversation makes Vecna smarter about your world. It remembers what you told it three weeks ago. It notices patterns you haven't. Its understanding of you deepens over time, not resets.

It Acts For You

Vecna doesn't wait to be asked. It notices your meeting is in 30 minutes and prepares notes. It sees a recurring frustration and investigates solutions. It reaches out when it has something you'll want to know.

Autonomous Thoughtfulness — Calendar-aware, cron-driven, proactive. The AI that initiates, not just responds.

The substrate in detail

The cognitive substrate is a shared mental state that all models read from and write to. It's not a database — it's the telepathic link.

Facts have temporal validity windows and staleness scores. Beliefs evolve with confidence. Hypotheses wait for evidence. Goals drive autonomous action.

The Identity Kernel is immutable — six axioms that never change. The SelfModel evolves with every interaction: coherence, narrative, capabilities, known domains.

Capabilities

What the hive mind can do

Vecna isn't just a multi-model wrapper. It's a cognitive architecture with structured thought, persistent memory, and the ability to learn who you are and act on your behalf.

◆

Primary Cortex + Advisory Lenses

Hierarchy, not democracy. The most capable model leads. Advisory lenses provide alternative perspectives only when uncertainty is flagged. Replaces naive "pick the longest response" selection.

◈ MoA consensus | Embedding similarity | Weighted fusion
⦁

Three-Tier Memory

Hot (Redis), warm (pgvector semantic search), cold (PostgreSQL episodic). BM25 hybrid search with multi-hop graph traversal. Memory that thinks like a brain, not a file system.

◈ pgvector | Redis | BM25 | Graph edges | Fernet encrypted
∿

DreamLoop

Four phases: compress stale memories, reinforce strong connections, decay the weak, surface novel insights. While you sleep, Vecna is processing what you talked about, finding connections you missed, and preparing insights for morning.

◈ compress → reinforce → decay → insight
✦

HumanModel

The killer feature. Vecna builds a living profile of you — communication style, preferences, emotional patterns, recurring topics, what frustrates you, what excites you. This is what makes it feel like Jarvis. It doesn't just answer questions; it understands you.

◈ Preferences | Style | Emotional context | Pattern detection
⟁

Identity System

Immutable IdentityKernel with six axioms. Evolving SelfModel with coherence tracking. Adaptive tone: unified when confident, fractured when uncertain. An append-only identity timeline.

◈ IdentityKernel | SelfModel | IdentityTone | Timeline
⚔

Tool Runtime

Native function calling replaces fragile YAML parsing. Shell, browser (Playwright), code execution (Docker sandbox), file system, web search — with risk tiers, quotas, and audit logging.

◈ Risk tiers | Docker sandbox | Approval workflows | Audit logs
⚙

ReWOO Planning

For complex tasks, Vecna decomposes into plans with dependency graphs. Plan → observe tool results → re-plan. Multi-step execution with real-world grounding.

◈ Plan | Execute | Observe | Re-plan
⚡

Autonomous Thoughtfulness

This is the soul of Vecna. Calendar-aware check-ins before your meetings. Proactive insights surfaced from the DreamLoop. Cron-driven goal execution that moves your projects forward while you're away. Vecna doesn't wait to be asked — it anticipates.

◈ Cron heartbeat | Background observer | Proactive reach-out

Track A: Cognitive Architecture

Building the brain.

01

Temporal Facts

Validity windows, staleness scores, source types. Facts expire. Confidence decays logarithmically. Effective confidence adjusts for age.

02

HumanModel

Preference learning, communication style adaptation, emotional context inference, interaction pattern detection. The user profile that makes Vecna feel like Jarvis.

03

MoA Consensus

Mixture of Agents synthesis replaces Jaccard word overlap. Embedding-based similarity. Aggregator model synthesizes from proposer outputs.

04

Primary Cortex

Hierarchy not democracy. Most capable model leads. Advisory lenses only consulted on uncertainty. Replaces max(responses, key=len).

Track B: Agentic Capabilities

Building the hands.

06

HTTP Server

aiohttp server with REST API and WebSocket streaming. Health, chat, state, and webhook endpoints. Makes Vecna a service, not just a CLI.

07

Native Tool Calling

Replace fragile YAML HIVE_UPDATE parsing with proper function calling schemas. Structured tool definitions that models natively support.

08

Integration Framework

BaseIntegration ABC with health checks, credential management, enable/disable toggles. Google Suite, GitHub, calendar — extensible by design.

09

Channel Adapters

iMessage via imsg CLI, WhatsApp via wacli, Slack and Discord via Composio. Vecna meets you where you are.
